Free & Open Source

NyxOS is Free, and Stays Free

Every line of NyxOS is GPL-2.0 and always will be — the kernel, the desktop, the browser, and the compiler. Nothing is paywalled, and nothing ever will be. This page exists for those who want to fund real hardware and hosting.

Help That Is Worth More Than Money

An operating system with no test reports is just a toy demo. These actions move NyxOS forward significantly — and cost nothing.

1

Boot on Physical Hardware

Burn NyxOS.iso to a USB flash drive and boot a secondary PC. Report where initialization halts.

2

File Actionable Issues

3

Star & Send Patches

Give the repository a star on GitHub, share screenshots, or submit a pull request.
